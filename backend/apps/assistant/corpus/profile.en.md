# Profile, password and language
URL: /profile

Your profile gathers your personal information (name, job title, department), your photo and your training transcript (completed courses and certificates). Edit your information and avatar from the "Profile" page.

To change your password while signed in, use the dedicated section of the profile: enter your old password, then the new one. If you forgot it, use "Forgot password" on the login page: a reset link is sent to you by e-mail.

The interface is available in French and English: switch with the application's language selector. You can also manage your e-mail notification preferences from the profile.
