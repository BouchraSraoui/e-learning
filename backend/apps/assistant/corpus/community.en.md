# Community: comments, reactions and chat rooms
URL: /community

Every course has a comment space: ask questions, reply to other learners (one reply level) and react with emojis. You can also rate a course from 1 to 5 stars and leave a review from its page — one review per person, editable.

The "Community" page offers real-time chat rooms between learners and trainers. Pick a room and write your message: it appears instantly for the other participants.

Exchanges are moderated: administrators and managers can hide an inappropriate comment or message, and ban a participant from a room. Stay courteous and professional.
