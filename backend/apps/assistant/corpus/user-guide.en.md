# User guide
URL: /dashboard

Overview of a learner's journey — after signing in you land on "Dashboard", and the path stays the same: find a course in "Course catalog", enroll, work through the lessons in the player, pass the quizzes, then collect your certificate. The navigation shows "Dashboard", "Course catalog" and "My learning"; the "More" menu gathers "Certificates", "Badges", "Leaderboard", "Community" and "Assistant".

Signing in to the platform — on the login page, enter your "Work email" and your "Password", tick "Keep me signed in" if you wish, then click "Sign in". Without an account, use "Create an account": "Full name", "Work email", "Department" and "Password". The "Continue with company SSO" button is not active yet; it only displays a message.

Reading your dashboard — the "Resume learning" button at the top of the page reopens the in-progress course you viewed most recently, or the catalog if you have none. Four counters show "Courses in progress", "Courses completed", "Certificates earned" and "Learning hours". Then come the "Continue learning", "Assigned to you", "Required for you", "Recommended for you" and "Recent certificates" sections.

Finding a course in the catalog — open "Course catalog", type keywords in the search field, then narrow the results with the "Category", "Level", "Format", "Duration" and "Sort by" lists. "Clear filters" resets everything and the "Grid" and "List" buttons change the layout; "Back" and "Next" page through the results.

Opening a course page and enrolling — click a card in the catalog. The page shows "About this course", "What you'll learn" and "Syllabus", and on the side the level, the duration, either "Certificate of completion" or "No certificate issued", then "Prerequisites" and "Resources". The "Enroll in course" button enrolls you and opens the player; it then becomes "Continue learning".

Recognising an assigned or required course — when a manager assigns you a course you are enrolled automatically: it appears under "Assigned to you" with "Assigned by …" and, where set, "Due …", and it also shows in "My learning". Some training is granted automatically to all staff or to a department. Courses carrying the "Required training" badge stay under "Required for you" until you complete them.

Working through lessons in the player — from "My learning", the "Start", "Resume" or "Review" button opens it. The course outline, chapter by chapter, sits on the side, followed by an "Assessment" section listing the quizzes. At the end of a lesson click "Mark complete & continue"; "Previous" and "Next" move through the outline.

Taking a quiz from the player — open it in the "Assessment" section of the outline, or through the "Take the quiz" button offered after the last lesson. The header states "Pass mark: … %". Answer every question then click "Submit answers". After a failed attempt, "Retake quiz" starts a new one.

Earning and verifying your certificate — once every lesson is complete and every quiz passed, the player shows "Course complete!"; if the course issues a certificate, a "View certificate" button appears as soon as it is created, otherwise the player says this course issues no certificate. On the "Certificates" page each card carries a "Certificate code", a "Download PDF" button and a "Verify" button that opens the public verification page, viewable without an account.

Tracking your badges and your rank — the "Badges" page shows "Badges earned" and "Badge points", then the whole catalog: badges you have not unlocked yet appear greyed out and locked. The "Leaderboard" page is read through the "Whole company" and "My department" tabs; your row is highlighted and tagged "You".

Commenting on a course and rating it — at the bottom of the course page, the "Discussion" section lets you write a message and click "Post", "Reply" to a comment and react with the "React" button. In the right-hand column, "Ratings & reviews" shows the average rating: pick 1 to 5 stars under "Rate this course", then "Submit review".

Chatting in the community rooms — open "Community" from the "More" menu. The "Rooms" column lists the rooms with their message count; pick one, type in the "Write a message…" field, then press Enter or the send button. You can delete your own messages.

Checking your notifications — the bell in the top bar carries a dot with the number of unread notifications and opens the most recent ones; "View all notifications" opens the "Notifications" page. Clicking a notification marks it as read and takes you to the page it concerns: a course, a certificate, a badge or a reply to a comment.

Editing your profile, your photo and your language — open "My profile" from your avatar menu. The "Overview" tab gathers your statistics and your completed courses; the "Settings" tab lets you correct "First name", "Last name", "Job title", "Phone" and "About", set "Interface language" and "Email notifications", then "Save changes". The button on the photo changes your avatar (JPG or PNG, 2 MB maximum).

Changing your password — it cannot be changed from inside the application. Sign out with "Sign out", then click "Forgot?" next to the "Password" field on the login page: enter your work email, confirm with "Send reset link" and follow the emailed link to set a new password.

Asking the assistant for help — the round button at the bottom right opens the "Learning Assistant" panel from any page; the "Assistant" entry in the "More" menu shows it full screen, with suggestions and a "New conversation" button. Answers cite their "Sources" and a notice reminds you that they can be imprecise.
