# Badges, points, streak and leaderboard
URL: /badges

The platform rewards consistent learning. You earn points as you learn, and badges at milestones: first course completed, quizzes passed, community participation, and so on. The "Badges" page lists the badge catalog and the ones you have unlocked, each worth points.

Your streak counts consecutive days of learning activity — it appears in your statistics.

The "Leaderboard" ranks employees by total points (learning points + badge points). You can view the company-wide ranking or your department's, and your own row is highlighted with your rank.
