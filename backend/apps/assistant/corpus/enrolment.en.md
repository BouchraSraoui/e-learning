# Enrolling in a course and finding your training
URL: /catalog

Open the Catalog to browse all published courses. You can search by keyword and filter by category, level, format (video, audio, PDF, slides, text) or duration. Click a course to see its page: description, objectives, chapters and lessons, downloadable resources and prerequisites.

To enrol, click "Enroll" on the course page. The course immediately appears in "My Learning", where your enrolments are grouped by status: all, in progress, completed.

Your manager can also assign a course to you: it then shows up automatically in "My Learning". Courses marked "mandatory" must be completed by all concerned employees; the ones you still have to do are highlighted on your dashboard.
