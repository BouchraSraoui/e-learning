# Progress, resuming and completing a course
URL: /my-learning

Your progress is saved automatically while you learn: completed lessons, playback position in videos and time spent. If you leave a course, you resume exactly where you stopped, from "My Learning" or the "Resume" button on the dashboard.

A course's progress bar reflects completed lessons and passed quizzes. A course is complete when all its lessons are marked done and all its published quizzes are passed. Mark a lesson as done with the button in the player.

Lessons are viewed directly inside the platform: videos and audio with resume, PDF documents and slide decks in the built-in viewer, and rich-text lessons. Files the browser cannot preview (for example PowerPoint) offer a download button instead.
