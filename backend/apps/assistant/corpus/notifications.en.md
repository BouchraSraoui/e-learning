# Notifications and reminders
URL: /notifications

The bell at the top of the screen shows your unread notifications: course enrolment, certificate earned, badge unlocked, reply to a comment, course assigned by your manager. The "Notifications" page lists the full history; mark one as read, or everything at once with "Mark all as read".

Depending on your preferences, important notifications are also sent by e-mail. You can turn notification e-mails on or off from your profile.

The platform also sends automatic reminders: after a long period of inactivity, and for mandatory training you haven't finished yet.
