# Quizzes and assessments
URL: /my-learning

Courses can include quizzes, attached to the course or to a chapter. Questions are single choice, multiple choice, true/false or dropdown. Launch a quiz from the course player; on the last lesson, a "Go to quiz" button takes you there directly.

Grading is automatic and immediate: you get your score, a pass or fail verdict against the required minimum score, and a question-by-question review. Quizzes can be retaken without limit: if you fail, click "Try again".

Note: the correct answers are only revealed once you pass the quiz. Passing all of a course's published quizzes counts towards its progress and is required for the certificate.
