# Getting started on the platform
URL: /dashboard

The Icosnet Training Library is the company's internal learning platform. Sign in with your work e-mail address and password from the login page. If you don't have an account yet, use "Create an account": fill in your name, e-mail, department and a password.

After signing in you land on your dashboard. It shows your learning statistics (courses in progress, completed courses, certificates, points), courses to resume, the mandatory training you still have to complete, recommendations and your recent certificates.

The main navigation gives access to the Catalog (all courses), My Learning (your enrolments), Certificates, Badges, the Leaderboard, the Community and Notifications. If you forgot your password, click "Forgot password" on the login page.
