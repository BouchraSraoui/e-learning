# Certificates: earning, downloading and verifying
URL: /certificates

A certificate is issued automatically when you complete a course that awards one: all lessons done and all quizzes passed. You receive a notification, and your manager is informed.

Find your certificates on the "Certificates" page. Each certificate can be downloaded as a PDF. It carries a unique tamper-evident verification code: anyone can confirm its authenticity by entering that code on the public verification page (/verify), no account needed.

Your certificates also appear on your profile (training transcript) and the most recent ones on your dashboard. Note: some courses, at their author's discretion, do not award a certificate; the course page says so.
